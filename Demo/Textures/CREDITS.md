### Equipment icons

- `Icons/Weapons/` and `Icons/Armor/` are rendered in-project from the demo's own equipment
  models with **Tools → Equipment Icon Generator**. They carry no attribution of their own
  beyond that of the models they are rendered from.

### Sign emblems

- `T_Emblem_Coins.png` is drawn for this project — five struck coins, matching the diamond
  faces of the Fantasy Props MegaKit's own coin models. It exists because none of that pack's
  six sign emblems is a bank. Generated, not hand-painted, so it can be redrawn at any size;
  it carries no third-party attribution.
